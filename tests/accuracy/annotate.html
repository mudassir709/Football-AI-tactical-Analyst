How to annotate

  1. Click the feet (the point on the grass between both feet) of every person whose feet you can see, with the right label. Do not skip anyone.
  2. "Team 1" and "Team 2" can be either team, but keep them the same for all frames of a clip.
  3. Click the centre of the ball if you can see it.
  4. For landmarks, click a pitch point that is clearly visible (corner of a box, end of the halfway line…) and type its number from the picture below. 3–6 per frame is enough.
  5. Right-click a mark to delete it; Z removes the last one. ←/→ change the frame.
  6. Press Save when you are done, and put the downloaded annotations.json in tests/accuracy/.
